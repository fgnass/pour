Pour

An acrylic open cup pour, on your phone.

  1. Pour a puddle of white.
  2. Set the cup into it.
  3. Pour colours into the cup. They sink under the white.
  4. Drag the cup off in a spiral, then lift it.
  5. Move the torch over the paint to make cells.
  6. Tilt your phone until the paint covers the canvas.

Tilt only acts while the Tilt tool is on. The pose at the moment you turn it on counts as flat.
